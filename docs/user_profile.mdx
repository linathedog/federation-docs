Open **User Information** from the user menu to inspect the identity information
Federation Registry received from the authentication proxy. Depending on the
tenant, this can include your unique identifier, name, username, email and
verification status, assurance information, and entitlements.

#### User Information page

![User Information page](/img/screenshots/user_info.png)

This information is read-only in Federation Registry. If it is incorrect or
missing, update it at its source identity provider or contact the tenant's
support team. Roles and allowed actions are derived from configured identity
attributes; the presence of a particular value does not by itself guarantee an
action unless the tenant maps it to that permission.
