To manage an existing service, a user must belong to its owners group. The
registration requester is added automatically; a group manager can invite
additional owners by email from the service's **Manage Owners** page.

## Accept or decline an invitation

1. Open the invitation link from the email.
2. Log in through the authentication proxy if necessary. Use the same identity
   to which the invitation should be linked.
3. Review the service and group role in the Invitations page.
4. Select **Accept** to join the owners group or **Decline** to reject it.

#### Invitations page with accept and decline actions

![Invitations page with accept and decline actions](/img/screenshots/invitations_page.png)

Pending invitations also appear as a notification above the Manage Services
list. An already activated invitation remains available from the Invitations
page until you respond.

#### Pending-invitation notification

![Pending-invitation notification](/img/screenshots/invitations_notification.png)

Accepting an invitation gives the permissions associated with the group role
and instance configuration. A group manager can invite or remove other members;
a regular member cannot manage membership. See
[Manage ownership of a service](service_list#manage-ownership-of-a-service).

If a link has expired, ask a group manager to renew or resend the invitation.
